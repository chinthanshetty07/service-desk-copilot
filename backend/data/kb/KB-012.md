id: KB-012
title: Report a suspicious or phishing email
category: Security
automation:
---
Use this when someone receives a suspicious email, or has clicked a link or opened an attachment from one.

Resolution steps:
1. Do not reply, click links or open attachments in the email.
2. Use the "Report phishing" button in Outlook to send it to the Security team.
3. If you clicked a link or entered your password, reset your password immediately and tell Security; this is a P1 security incident.
4. Delete the email after reporting it.
