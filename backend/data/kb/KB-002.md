---
id: KB-002
title: Account locked out after failed sign-in attempts
category: Access & Identity
automation: account_unlock
---
Accounts lock for 30 minutes after 10 failed sign-in attempts. Locked-out users see "Your account is locked" on the sign-in page.

Resolution steps:
1. Confirm the lockout in the directory and note the source of the failed attempts.
2. If the failures come from an old phone or laptop with a saved password, ask the user to update or remove it.
3. Unlock the account after identity verification, or wait 30 minutes for the automatic unlock.
4. If failures come from an unknown location, treat it as a possible compromise and escalate to Security.

Automation: unlocking can be automated after identity verification when the failures come from the user's own devices.
