id: KB-009
title: Printer offline or not printing
category: Hardware
automation:
---
Use this when print jobs are stuck, the printer shows offline, or nothing comes out.

Resolution steps:
1. Check the printer has paper and toner and shows no error on its display.
2. Clear stuck jobs from the print queue on the laptop.
3. Remove and re-add the printer from the printer list using its floor name.
4. If the printer is offline for everyone, report it with the printer name and floor.
