id: KB-003
title: Set up or reset multi-factor authentication (MFA)
category: Access & Identity
automation:
---
Use this when someone has a new phone, lost their authenticator app, or is not receiving MFA prompts.

Resolution steps:
1. Verify the user's identity by video call or in person; MFA resets cannot be approved over email.
2. Remove the old authenticator registration from the user's account.
3. Ask the user to sign in and register the authenticator app on the new phone.
4. Confirm they receive a test prompt before closing the ticket.
