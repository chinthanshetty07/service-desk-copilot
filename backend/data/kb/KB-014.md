How tickets are prioritised and how quickly the service desk responds.

P1 Critical: a service is down for many users, or a security incident such as a lost device or a clicked phishing link. Response within 15 minutes.
P2 High: one user cannot work, for example locked out or unable to connect. Response within 1 hour.
P3 Normal: a problem with a workaround, or a request such as new software or access. Response within 1 working day.
