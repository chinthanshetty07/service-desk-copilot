title: Lost or stolen laptop or phone
category: Security
automation:
---
Use this when a company laptop or phone is lost or stolen.

Resolution steps:
1. Report it immediately; lost devices with company data are P1 security incidents.
2. Security will remotely lock the device and sign it out of all company apps.
3. File a police report for stolen devices and attach the reference to the ticket.
4. A replacement device is issued after the incident is logged.
