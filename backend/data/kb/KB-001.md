id: KB-001
title: Reset a forgotten password
category: Access & Identity
automation: password_reset
---
Use this when an employee has forgotten their password or their password has expired.

Resolution steps:
1. Ask the employee to open the self-service password portal and choose "Forgot password".
2. Verify identity with the registered MFA method or a one-time code sent to the recovery phone.
3. Set a new password of at least 14 characters that has not been used before.
4. Sign out of all devices and sign back in so mail and VPN pick up the new password.

Automation: password resets can be completed automatically after identity verification. Never reset a password based on an email request alone.
