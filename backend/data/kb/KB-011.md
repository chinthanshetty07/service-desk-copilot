id: KB-011
title: Microsoft Teams audio or video not working
category: Email & Collaboration
automation:
---
Use this when others cannot hear you in Teams, your camera does not show, or calls drop.

Resolution steps:
1. In Teams settings, choose the correct microphone, speaker and camera under Devices.
2. Check the operating system has given Teams permission to use the camera and microphone.
3. Close other apps that use the camera, such as Zoom or the camera app.
4. Update Teams and restart the laptop if the problem continues.
