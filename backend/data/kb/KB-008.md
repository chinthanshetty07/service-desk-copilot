title: Laptop running slow or freezing
category: Hardware
automation:
---
Use this when a laptop is slow, freezes, or the fan runs constantly.

Resolution steps:
1. Restart the laptop; many slowdowns clear after pending updates install.
2. Check free disk space and remove large downloads if less than 10 percent is free.
3. Open the task manager and note any app using high CPU or memory.
4. If the laptop is over four years old or still slow after these steps, request a hardware check.
