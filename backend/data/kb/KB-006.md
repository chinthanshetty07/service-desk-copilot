id: KB-006
title: Outlook not syncing or emails missing
category: Email & Collaboration
automation:
---
Use this when new emails are not arriving in Outlook, the inbox is stuck, or Outlook shows "Disconnected".

Resolution steps:
1. Check webmail in a browser; if mail is there, the problem is the Outlook app.
2. Restart Outlook and confirm it shows "Connected" in the status bar.
3. Clear the Outlook cache or rebuild the profile from the mail settings.
4. Check the mailbox is not over its storage quota and archive old items if it is.
